# Viya — Play Store & App Store Listing

## App Name
Viya — Your Life, Automated

## Short Description (80 chars)
Your AI life agent for money, habits & health — in the app or on WhatsApp.

## Full Description

**Viya is an AI life agent, not just an app** — talk to it naturally about your money, health, and habits, in the app or on WhatsApp, and it acts on what you actually meant.

🏦 **SMART MONEY MANAGEMENT**
• Log expenses and income just by typing or saying them naturally — "spent 500 on swiggy," no forms
• Scan a bill photo and Viya reads the amount and category for you
• AI-suggested daily and monthly budgets based on your income, goals, and family size
• Bill and EMI reminders so you never miss a due date, with an advance heads-up
• Recurring subscription tracking
• Split bills with friends in one tap
• Track investments and SIPs alongside your everyday spending

💚 **HEALTH & WELLNESS HUB**
• Steps, water, sleep, weight & mood tracking
• Medicine reminders
• Meal logging with calorie estimation
• Sleep quality scoring

🔥 **HABIT BUILDER**
• Build any habit with visual streak tracking
• Streak-freeze tokens to protect your progress on an off day
• Celebratory milestones and achievement badges

🤖 **THE SAME AI, ON WHATSAPP**
• Just text "spent 500 on food" to log it — no app switching required
• Ask "how much left this month?" for an instant answer
• Daily morning briefs and weekly summaries sent to you
• Voice input support in the app

🧠 **GROUNDED AI, NOT GENERIC ADVICE**
• Viya's AI answers are grounded in your real transactions, goals, and bills — not a canned script
• It can explain *why* a goal might be stalling, e.g. a competing EMI eating the same budget
• Understands regional languages and mixed phrasing (Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Hinglish, Tanglish)

🏆 **PROGRESS THAT FEELS GOOD**
• Earn XP and level up as you build good habits
• Compete with friends on the leaderboard
• Unlock achievement badges

📊 **REPORTS & INSIGHTS**
• Monthly report with savings-rate grading and month-over-month comparison
• Category breakdowns and top-expense tracking
• Spending predictions based on your own patterns

**WHY VIYA?**
✅ Talk to it naturally — it's an agent, not a rigid command bot
✅ WhatsApp assistant included — no app switching needed
✅ Grounded in your real data, not generic financial advice
✅ 100% free to start
✅ Made in India 🇮🇳

## Notes on accuracy

This listing was audited against the actual codebase (2026-07) and every
claim above corresponds to a real, working feature — no aspirational or
dead-code claims (a prior version of this listing advertised bank-SMS
auto-tracking, AI journal mood analysis, and weekly challenges that had no
working implementation or UI; those have been removed to avoid a Play Store
policy strike or user complaints about false advertising). If a new feature
is added or removed, update this file at the same time as the code change.
